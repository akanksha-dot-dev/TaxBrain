## The Basics: What Gets Taxed

As a salaried employee, your employer pays you a salary. The government wants a cut of this income — that's income tax. But instead of collecting it all at year-end, they take it **monthly** from your paycheck. This monthly deduction is called **TDS** (Tax Deducted at Source).

## Key Terms (No Jargon)

| Term | What It Actually Means |
|---|---|
| **PAN** | Your 10-character tax identity number (like ABCDE1234F) |
| **TDS** | Tax your employer deducts from your salary every month |
| **ITR** | Income Tax Return — the form you file to tell the government your total income and tax |
| **Form 16 (now Form 130)** | Certificate from your employer showing salary paid and TDS deducted |
| **Assessment Year (AY)** | The year you FILE your return (AY 2027-28 = for income earned in FY 2026-27) |
| **Financial Year (FY)** | The year you EARNED the income (April to March) |
| **Tax Year** | New term under Income Tax Act 2025, replaces FY+AY concept |

> Under the Income Tax Act 2025 (effective April 1, 2026), "Financial Year" and "Assessment Year" are being replaced by **"Tax Year"**. FY 2026-27 = Tax Year 2026-27. The filing still happens in the following year.

## Your Tax Timeline

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **April** | New financial year begins. Submit investment declarations to employer |
| **June 15** | First advance tax installment (only if tax > ₹10,000 after TDS) |
| **January–March** | Submit investment proofs to employer for final TDS adjustment |
| **June** | Employer issues Form 16 (Form 130) |
| **July 31** | **ITR filing deadline** (for salaried individuals) |

## Which ITR Form?

| Form | Who Should Use |
|---|---|
| **ITR-1 (Sahaj)** | Salary + one house property + other income ≤ ₹50L total. Most salaried people use this |
| **ITR-2** | Salary + capital gains, or total income > ₹50L, or foreign assets |
| **ITR-3** | Business or professional income |

**If you're a salaried employee with income ≤ ₹50L and no capital gains, use ITR-1.**

## Filing Your First ITR: Step by Step

1. **Login to incometax.gov.in** with PAN and password
2. **Choose the correct AY** (Assessment Year 2027-28 for FY 2026-27)
3. **Select ITR form** (ITR-1 for most salaried individuals)
4. **Pre-filled data** — The portal auto-fills salary, TDS, and bank details from your employer and banks
5. **Verify pre-filled data** against your Form 16
6. **Choose your regime** — New regime (default) or Old regime
7. **Add deductions** — 80C, 80D, etc. (if choosing old regime)
8. **Compute tax** — The portal calculates. Compare with TDS deducted.
9. **Pay any shortfall** — If you owe additional tax, pay online before filing
10. **Submit & e-verify** — Use Aadhaar OTP for instant verification

## Common First-Timer Questions

### "My employer already deducted TDS. Why do I need to file?"

TDS is an **advance payment**, not the final word. Filing ITR reconciles your total income, total TDS, and actual tax liability. You might even get a **refund** if excess TDS was deducted.

### "What if I don't file?"

- Interest under Section 234A (1% per month)
- Late filing fee: ₹5,000 (₹1,000 if income ≤ ₹5L)
- Can't carry forward capital losses
- Refund gets delayed

### "Do I need a CA?"

For simple salaried returns (ITR-1), **no**. The incometax.gov.in portal is designed for self-filing. Save the CA fee (₹1,000–₹5,000) and do it yourself.

### "What about the new Income Tax Act 2025?"

The new act reorganizes section numbers and uses "Tax Year" instead of FY/AY. But the **tax rates, slabs, and deductions remain unchanged** for FY 2026-27. The key renaming:

| Old (1961 Act) | New (2025 Act) |
|---|---|
| Section 80C | Section 123 |
| Section 80D | Section 126 |
| Form 16 | Form 130 |
| Form 12BB | Form 124 |
