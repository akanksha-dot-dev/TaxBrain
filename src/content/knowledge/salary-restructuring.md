## What Is Salary Restructuring?

Your CTC (Cost to Company) stays the same. But HOW it's split between components changes your tax. Moving money from fully-taxable components (like Special Allowance) to exempt components (like NPS or meal vouchers) reduces your taxable income — **legally**.

## The Big Three Optimizations

### 1. Employer NPS (Saves ₹15,000–₹30,000/year)

**How it works:** Employer contributes to your NPS account. This portion is tax-exempt.

- New Regime: Up to **14% of Basic**
- Old Regime: Up to **10% of Basic** (private sector)

**Impact for ₹91,667/month Basic (8 months):**
```
Exempt amount: ₹91,667 × 14% × 8 = ₹1,02,667
Tax saved (20% slab): ~₹21,350
```

### 2. Meal Vouchers / Food Card (Saves ₹7,000–₹15,000/year)

Since **April 2026**, the meal voucher limit increased from ₹50 to **₹200 per meal**. This is a significant change.

**Calculation:**
```
₹200/meal × 2 meals/day × 22 working days × 12 months = ₹1,05,600/year
```

This amount is **fully tax-exempt in BOTH regimes** when provided through a non-transferable meal card (like Sodexo or similar).

> **Important:** The ₹200/meal limit is effective from April 1, 2026. Your employer must provide this through a designated meal card, not as cash.

### 3. Telephone/Internet Reimbursement (Saves ₹3,000–₹5,000/year)

If your employer reimburses phone and internet bills against actual bills:

```
₹1,500/month × 12 = ₹18,000/year tax-free
```

This requires submitting actual bills to your employer.

## Combined Savings Example

For someone at the 20% slab with ₹91,667/month Basic:

| Optimization | Annual Exempt | Tax Saved |
|---|---|---|
| Employer NPS (14%) | ₹1,54,000 | ~₹32,000 |
| Meal Vouchers | ₹1,05,600 | ~₹22,000 |
| Phone Reimbursement | ₹18,000 | ~₹3,750 |
| **Total** | **₹2,77,600** | **~₹57,750** |

> That's nearly ₹5,000/month more in your pocket — with zero additional investment.

## How to Ask Your HR

Most companies allow salary restructuring during:
- **Joining** (easiest time — negotiate during offer)
- **Annual appraisal cycle**
- **Mid-year** (some companies allow, others don't)

Key points for your conversation:
1. "I'd like to opt for employer NPS at 14% of Basic"
2. "Can we add meal voucher benefit under the new ₹200/meal limit?"
3. "Is telephone reimbursement available against bills?"
4. "This is within my existing CTC — no additional cost to the company"

## What About HRA?

HRA (House Rent Allowance) is a major exemption but only works in the **old regime**. If you're in the new regime, HRA is fully taxable — which is fine because the new regime's lower rates usually compensate.

For old regime users: HRA exemption = minimum of:
1. Actual HRA received
2. 50% of Basic (metro) or 40% of Basic (non-metro)
3. Rent paid minus 10% of Basic
