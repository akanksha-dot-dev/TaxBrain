## The Big Picture

The **Income Tax Act 2025** replaces the 1961 Act effective **April 1, 2026**. But here's the crucial point:

> **Tax rates, slabs, and deduction amounts are UNCHANGED.** The new act reorganizes the law, not the math.

Think of it as a "refactoring" of the tax code — same functionality, cleaner structure, new section numbers.

## Key Terminology Changes

| Old Term | New Term |
|---|---|
| Financial Year (FY) | **Tax Year** |
| Assessment Year (AY) | Eliminated (filing year is just "Tax Year + 1") |
| Previous Year | Eliminated |

So FY 2026-27 = **Tax Year 2026-27**. You file the return by July 31, 2027.

## Section Number Mapping

The most commonly used sections have new numbers:

| Old Section (1961 Act) | New Section (2025 Act) | What It Covers |
|---|---|---|
| **80C** | **123** | PPF, ELSS, EPF, LIC, tuition fees, etc. (₹1.5L limit) |
| **80CCD(1B)** | **123(2)** | Additional NPS self-contribution (₹50K) |
| **80CCD(2)** | **123(3)** | Employer NPS contribution (14%/10%) |
| **80D** | **126** | Health insurance premium |
| **80TTA** | **125** | Savings account interest (₹10K limit) |
| **24(b)** | **Sec 23(2)** | Home loan interest deduction (₹2L) |
| **10(13A)** | **Sec 17(3)** | HRA exemption |
| **87A** | **Sec 113** | Rebate for income ≤ ₹12L (new regime) |

## Form Renaming

| Old Form | New Form | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Form 16** | **Form 130** | Salary certificate from employer |
| **Form 12B** | **Form 124** | Declaration of previous employer income |
| **Form 12BB** | **Form 124** | Investment declaration to employer |
| **Form 26AS** | **Form 126** | Tax credit statement |

## What's Actually Different

### 1. Simplified Language
The new act uses plain English where the old act used legalese. For example, "income computed under the head salaries" is now simply "salary income."

### 2. Fewer Sections
The old act had 298 sections + many sub-sections. The new act consolidates these into ~280 sections with a logical hierarchy.

### 3. Tax Year Concept
No more FY/AY confusion. Everything is just "Tax Year 2026-27" — the year you earned the income.

### 4. Same Tax Rates
This is worth repeating: **the tax slabs, rates, deduction limits, and exemptions are identical.** The numbers in your payslip and ITR are the same — only the section references change.

## What This Means for You

1. **Nothing changes in your tax planning** — same strategies, same limits, same math
2. **Form numbers change** — ask your employer for "Form 130" instead of "Form 16"
3. **Section references in TaxBrain** — We use old section numbers (80C, 80D) for familiarity since most Indians know them. The calculator shows both old and new references where relevant.
4. **CA/advisor conversations** — Be prepared for both old and new section references during the transition period
