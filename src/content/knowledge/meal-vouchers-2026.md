## What Changed in 2026

Starting **April 1, 2026**, the meal voucher exemption limit increased from ₹50/meal to **₹200/meal**. This is a 4x increase that makes meal vouchers a genuinely valuable tax optimization.

## The Math

```
₹200 per meal × 2 meals per day × 22 working days × 12 months
= ₹1,05,600 per year — completely tax-free
```

For someone in the 20% tax slab, this saves approximately **₹22,000 in tax per year**.

> For a mid-year job switch (8 months at new employer):
> ₹200 × 2 × 22 × 8 = **₹70,400** tax-free

## Key Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Per meal limit** | ₹200 (not ₹200/day — each meal is separate) |
| **Meals per day** | Typically 2 (lunch + snacks/dinner) |
| **Working days** | Only working days count (not weekends/holidays) |
| **Delivery method** | Must be through a **non-transferable meal card** (Sodexo, Zeta, etc.) |
| **Cash reimbursement** | ❌ Not eligible — must be a designated meal card |
| **Both regimes** | ✅ Available in **both** New and Old regime |

## How to Set It Up

1. **Ask HR** if meal voucher benefit is available
2. **Specify the amount** — request ₹200/meal × 2 meals × 22 days = ₹8,800/month
3. **Salary restructuring** — This amount moves from your Special Allowance (taxable) to Meal Voucher (exempt)
4. **You'll receive a meal card** — pre-loaded monthly, usable at restaurants and food delivery apps

## Where Can You Use Meal Cards?

Most meal voucher cards work at:
- Restaurants and cafeterias
- Swiggy, Zomato (some cards)
- Grocery stores (some cards)
- Company cafeteria

> Check with your employer which meal card provider they use and where it's accepted.
