## Why NPS Is Special

NPS (National Pension System) is the **only deduction that works significantly in BOTH tax regimes**. While Section 80C and 80D are blocked in the new regime, employer NPS contribution under Section 80CCD(2) remains fully available.

## The Three NPS Tax Benefits

### 1. Employer NPS — Section 80CCD(2) ✅ Both Regimes

This is the **big one**. Your employer contributes to your NPS account, and this amount is exempt from tax:

| Regime | Limit |
|---|---|
| **New Regime** | **14% of Basic salary** |
| **Old Regime** | 10% of Basic salary (private sector) |

**Example (Basic = ₹91,667/month, 8 months at new job):**
```
New Regime: ₹91,667 × 14% × 8 = ₹1,02,667 tax-free
Old Regime: ₹91,667 × 10% × 8 = ₹73,333 tax-free
```

This single deduction can save ₹20,000–₹30,000 in tax annually.

### 2. Self NPS — Section 80CCD(1B) ❌ Old Regime Only

An **additional ₹50,000** deduction over and above the ₹1.5L 80C limit. Only available in old regime.

### 3. Employee NPS — Section 80CCD(1) ❌ Old Regime Only

Your own NPS contribution (up to 10% of salary). This is part of the ₹1.5L 80C aggregate limit, so it competes with EPF, ELSS, etc.

## How to Get Employer NPS

This is **not automatic**. You must:

1. **Ask your HR/payroll team** to set up employer NPS contribution
2. **Specify the percentage** (ask for the maximum: 14% in new regime)
3. **Your CTC doesn't change** — the employer restructures your salary. Some amount moves from "Special Allowance" (fully taxable) to "Employer NPS" (exempt).

>
> Hi [HR/Payroll Team],
>

>

>

## NPS vs PPF vs ELSS

| Factor | Employer NPS | Self NPS (1B) | PPF | ELSS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Works in New Regime** | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No |
| **Extra deduction** | Over and above 80C | ₹50K extra | Within 80C | Within 80C |
| **Lock-in** | Till 60 | Till 60 | 15 years | 3 years |
| **Returns** | 8-12% | 8-12% | 7.1% | 10-15% |
| **Tax at withdrawal** | 60% tax-free | 60% tax-free | Fully exempt | LTCG applies |

## The Bottom Line

For new regime users: **Employer NPS at 14% is your #1 tax optimization**. It requires zero investment from your pocket — it's a salary restructuring that reduces your taxable income.
